## Phase 1 — Scaffold + Gate G0 ⛔

**Goal:** a repo that runs, and an answer to the only question that can kill the project.

### Track A — Scaffold ✅
Project layout, `pyproject.toml`, licences (MIT + CC BY-SA), `.gitignore`, package skeleton, 14
passing tests, `knowledge.md` stale bullets corrected (findings F9), `AGENTS.md` updated.

### Track B — Gate G0 ⛔ failed
Matcher implemented and run against real data. Result: **181 high-confidence matches** against a
threshold of 300 to continue. Full diagnosis in `docs/label-matching-report.md` and findings F11.

Root cause is a data limit, not a matcher defect: GRID3 names only **869 corridors** across the
54,998 named highway segments, and 113,004 highway segments have no name at all. The named network
offers just 936 distinct place tokens, so 426 of the 797 unmatched events name roads that do not
exist in the network's vocabulary.

**Per the pre-committed rule: building stops here until the scope is re-decided.**

**Closed here:** findings O1 (match rate = 181/1,672 events; 66 corridors).

**Goal:** every static input in Parquet, versioned and reproducible, with the corridor table as the
modelling unit.

- ✅ GRID3 named highway segments (53,998) with state assignment.
- ✅ OCDS events normalised and typed (2,036 maintenance events; 1,672 with a match attempt).
- ✅ **Corridor table: 869 corridors, 18,419 km, 66 labelled, 36 with repeat events.**
- ✅ Corridor geometry: **869/869 corridors**, 127k vertices at 11 m tolerance, 2.74 MB raw /
  0.64 MB gzipped (findings F14).
- ✅ Hugging Face publish path: `hf` module, `scripts/publish_dataset.py`, parquet + generated
  dataset card, `.github/workflows/pipeline.yml` rebuilding from sources and uploading.
- ✅ Re-classification of all candidates by year × intervention type (findings F13).

- ✅ DuckDB views over the parquet, verified locally: 869 corridors, 66 labelled, 18,419.1 km,
  2,036 events, 181 high-confidence matches (`scripts/check_warehouse.py`).

**Exit criterion:** a DuckDB query returns 869 corridors and 2,036 events; a fresh clone can fetch
the tables over HTTPS; upload runs in CI. **Met on the build side.** The first real upload needs
`HF_TOKEN`, the only credential this project requires, and it is a one-time manual step — the
workflow fails loudly rather than skipping silently if it is absent.

## Phase 3 — Imagery + weather 🔄

**Goal:** prove the sampler, then scale it.

- ✅ Tile arithmetic (`tiles.py`), validated against a real STAC transform.
- ✅ STAC discovery and anonymous COG reads (`sampler.py`).
- ✅ Exact byte accounting (`netcount.py`) — a counting proxy, because GDAL exposes no counter.
- ✅ National tile census: **2,016 tiles, 97 items** (findings F18, closes O2).
- ✅ One-state pilot on Lagos, run twice: **646–744 KB and 10.7–30.5 s per tile-month at 40 m**,
  national projection **31–36 GB / 143–410 h serial** (findings F17). `docs/pilot-report.md` is
  generated by the script, so it always matches the last run.
- ✅ **Cost model corrected** — F2's "10× cheaper at 20 m" was wrong by ~9×; measured 857 KB at
  20 m against 934 KB at 10 m (findings F16). Default resolution is now 40 m.
- ⬜ Weather from Open-Meteo per 1° cell.
- ⬜ Backfill runner: sharded across parallel jobs, resumable, since 143 h serial does not fit one.
- ⬜ **Feature redesign from F17:** centreline NDVI measures asphalt (≈ 0), so Phase 4 needs a
  road-versus-verge contrast rather than a single centreline band.

**Exit criterion:** pilot features for one state; measured bytes/requests recorded in
`docs/findings.md` replacing the 6,000-tile estimate (closes O2). **Met** on imagery; weather and
the sharded backfill remain, and the backfill plan depends on the F17 runtime (the bytes are stable, the runtime is not).

**Open decision before the national run:** 40 m (31 GB) or 10 m (~68 GB). The pilot supports 40 m
on cost, but O3's ablation should confirm the two rank corridors similarly before committing.

## Phase 4 — Features, Feast, dbt ⏳

**Goal:** turn observations into a versioned feature set with tests.

- 24-month rolling NDVI/NDBI statistics, rainfall and soil-moisture features.
- dbt models with tests and docs; Feast feature views over Parquet.
- Data-quality checks emitted as metrics.

**Exit criterion:** `dbt build` green including tests; `feast apply` + historical retrieval returns
the expected matrix for a sample of segments.

---

## Phase 5 — Modelling ⏳

**Goal:** a defensible hazard model and an honest secondary survival analysis.

- Discrete-time hazard, temporal split (train 2023–2024, test 2025).
- Baselines: class-only, and a no-satellite feature set.
- MLflow tracking; calibration; top-decile capture.
- Secondary: Cox PH + RSF with Schoenfeld diagnostics, explicitly labelled as thin.

**Exit criterion:** AUC ≥ 0.70 and ≥ +0.05 over the class-only baseline, or a published explanation of
why the signal is weak. Model card written.

---

## Phase 6 — Optimisation ⏳

**Goal:** turn risk into an allocatable plan.

- 0/1 knapsack (PuLP/CBC) over predicted risk × exposure, subject to budget.
- Sensitivity across budget levels; improvement multiplier vs uniform allocation.
- Both exposure proxies (global AADT, catchment population) reported.

**Exit criterion:** allocation table + marginal-value curve; results reproducible from CI.
