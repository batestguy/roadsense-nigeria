**Verdict: 40 m preserves the level index but **not** the contrast feature — run the condition signal at 10 m.**

Three correlations are reported because they answer different questions. *Level* is what a
cross-sectional index needs. *Change* is what a degradation signal needs. *Contrast* is what
separates a maintained corridor from a failing one, and it is the test a coarse resolution
is most likely to fail: a 40 m pixel containing a road is a road-plus-verge mixture, so the
difference between carriageway and verge can vanish or invert even when levels still rank.
