The road condition signal does not clear its own bar

One page on what this project measured across 850 federal road corridors, why the measurement failed its pre-specified test, and what it still supports.

The result is not "the effect is small". It is that the instrument ranks the labelled corridors the wrong way round, so it cannot be used to allocate maintenance, and this page exists to say so before anything else on it.

What the four charts show

How the measurement was made

For each corridor and each month, the mean normalised difference vegetation index of a band along the carriageway is subtracted from the mean of the verge beside it, at 10 metre resolution, from Sentinel-2 via the DE Africa platform. One value per corridor per month, 18,700 corridor-months over 22 months, with 183 corridor-months missing and left missing.

The primary instrument asks whether the contrast falls faster on corridors with a procurement record than on comparable corridors without one. Corridors are matched exactly on road class and on length band within class; the comparison is the probability that a labelled corridor ranks as more degraded than an unlabelled one, with a bootstrap interval and a permutation null.

Three things this does not claim.

  1. Movement, not condition. The score has no level. A corridor that was always bad and a corridor that never changed can produce the same value, so nothing here ranks current condition.
  2. Reanalysis, not gauges. The rainfall used for the weather covariate is a gridded reanalysis product, not a rain gauge, and it is used as context rather than as a measured cause.
  3. Observational, not causal. 66 labelled corridors is a small, non-random, spending-driven sample. A procurement record is evidence that money was allocated, never that a road failed.

What was checked

  • Completeness. Imagery: 22 of 22 months present, 0 missing, 69,212 rows, 850 corridors, 0 duplicate keys. Weather: 18,700 rows, 0 missing, 0 nulls.
  • Duplicate keys. The merge is keyed on corridor, tile and month; the count above is asserted at zero rather than assumed.
  • The degeneracy gate. Before any test, each instrument is checked for having enough distinct values and no modal value holding more than a fifth of the sample. All three passed: Primary 850 distinct values on the modal share of 0.1%, Secondary 850 distinct, Reference 850 distinct.
  • The coverage failure mode. A score that was really a proxy for coverage produced a correlation of -0.0123 and an AUC of 0.5089 when coverage itself was used as the score, so the reported instruments are not reading data availability.
  • The bar. The decision rule was written down before the test ran: point estimate at or above 0.60 and lower bound above 0.50. It was not met, and it was not lowered afterwards.
